NAS-BRCA-002 PAM50 biological artwork v2

Created for NaS Research as microscopy-informed biological illustrations.
Composition and biological layout were developed with editable Blender 5.2.0
underlays, then finalized through an image-generation-assisted illustration pass
to achieve organic tissue and intracellular detail.

Master dimensions: 1672 x 941 pixels, PNG.
Web dimensions: 1672 x 941 pixels, WebP.

The cover depicts a conceptual field of irregular breast-cancer cells with
heterogeneous nuclei and extracellular matrix. The article image moves inside a
conceptual tumor cell to show a nucleus, nucleolus, chromatin-like texture,
organelles, nuclear pores, and single-stranded RNA transcripts. Together they
provide biological context for a gene-expression study.

The images are not patient tissue or microscopy data. They are not observed
molecular structures, literal PAM50 molecules, empirical data plots, or
additional study results. RNA strands and signal highlights do not identify
particular genes or encode expression values, subtype scores, patients, effects,
or clinical outcomes.

Editable Blender composition underlays and lossless selected masters are
preserved in the internal NAS-BRCA-002 visual archive. Website derivatives are
stored with the public research release assets.
