NAS-BRCA-002 PAM50 conceptual artwork v1

Created by NaS Research in Blender 5.2.0 LTS.
Renderer: Blender EEVEE, 128 samples.
Master resolution: 7680 x 4320 pixels, 16-bit PNG.
Web resolution: 2400 x 1350 pixels, WebP.

The cover contains exactly 50 rounded markers for the fixed 50-gene panel and
five concentric rings for the five fixed PAM50 subtype references. The method
artwork contains two sets of exactly 50 markers and 50 connecting curves to
represent repeated measurements of the same panel. Its five central lenses
represent the five fixed subtype references.

These images are explanatory conceptual artwork. They are not observed molecular
structures, empirical data plots, breast-tissue images, patient information, or
additional study results. Marker position, color, brightness, and size do not
encode a particular gene, patient, subtype, score, effect, or outcome.

Editable Blender scenes and lossless masters are preserved in the internal
NAS-BRCA-002 visual archive. Website derivatives are stored with the public
research release assets.
