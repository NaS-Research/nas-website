# Public research presentation

Routine production-tool disclosure belongs in the website-wide tools disclosure.
Do not repeat OpenAI, Codex, LLM, or AI-assistance credits in research articles,
PDFs, footers, or companion-package front matter. Keep publication builders and
release-administration material private.

Preserve scientific methods, model/software names that are part of the work,
source attribution, limitations, and accurate authorship. Never imply review or
validation that has not occurred. Editorial revisions leave the results intact,
receive a new version, and preserve previous editions in the external archive.

All revised papers use the shared NaS publication renderer and palette at
`../NaS-Core/workflows/publications/nas_paper_v1/README.md`.
