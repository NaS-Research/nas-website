## LinkedIn

Today, NaS Research is introducing NaS Denials: a software foundation under development for specialty denial prevention and human-reviewed appeals.

Claims work is often fragmented across clinical records, remittance files, payer policies, portals, faxes, and manual queues. NaS Denials is designed to turn each denial into an accountable case—connecting the payer rule, supporting and missing evidence, human decisions, appeal materials, and verified payment outcomes.

We are publishing the work before customer results exist. The current repository passes local engineering tests with synthetic data, but the product has not yet been validated with a customer, authorized for an ePHI deployment, or shown to recover money in practice.

That is the next stage.

We are looking for specialty practices, revenue-cycle leaders, billing companies, and qualified denial reviewers interested in defining one narrow retrospective validation cohort, followed—if the evidence supports it—by a prospective shadow pilot.

An initial conversation does not require patient information.

Read the release and design-partner plan: https://nasresearch.bio/research/introducing-nas-denials
