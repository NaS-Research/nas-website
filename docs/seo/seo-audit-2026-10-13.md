## Content, semantics and scientific trust

- Titles and descriptions were already unique and descriptive across the live sitemap. They were preserved instead of mechanically rewritten.
- The home carousel has multiple H1 elements in source, with inactive slides marked aria-hidden and inert. No demonstrated heading defect justified an SEO-only rewrite.
- Representative images were inspected for alt attributes. Existing publication artwork has descriptive alt text. No automatic keyword additions were made.
- All 232 module records resolve references (2,985 entries); all 300 drug monographs have sources (1,208 entries). All 6,911 drug disclosure blocks have citations, and source identifier checks found no unknown IDs. The two standalone guides contain 3 and 4 sources respectively.
- These are structural provenance checks, not an independent review of the medical claims or source currency.
- The 11 research articles have visible bylines. Current structured author types match the present data: two named people with affiliations and nine organizational bylines.
- Drug/module/guide records do not establish verified individual author/reviewer identities. Adding truthful bylines, reviewer qualifications and genuine review dates requires the content owner's evidence. No person, credential, review, rating or medical claim was invented.
- Existing Organization, WebSite, Article, LearningResource and ResearchProject structured data were reviewed for consistency with content. Schema parsing is tested; this is not a claim of rich-result eligibility or Google validation.
- Existing real publication/revision dates were retained. No synthetic lastmod or review date was set to the build date.

## Performance interpretation

Using identical HTTP user-agent and Accept-Encoding: identity settings, the live /research response was 281,799 bytes and the local patched response was 39,416 bytes: 242,383 bytes less, an 86.01% reduction in uncompressed response size. These are observed live-versus-local measurements, not a same-host latency benchmark. Research card rendering and interactions passed on both viewports.

This change removes unnecessary research listing serialization. It does not establish improved field Core Web Vitals. No Search Console, CrUX or equivalent authorized field dataset was available for this task. Assess LCP, INP and CLS at the 75th percentile after an authorized release.

Large educational pages remain a performance opportunity. The patched local route audit measured uncompressed responses of 959,158 bytes for toxicology, 677,859 bytes for hypertension, 665,792 bytes for pregnancy/lactation, and 626,345 bytes for asthma. These contain extensive scientific content and interactive presentation; reducing payload should be a separate template-level effort coordinated with the Learn owner, preserving accessible server-rendered content, diagrams and citations.
