## Immutable blueprint

`src/data/drugMonographs/acetaminophen.js` defines the content. `DrugMonograph.jsx`, `DrugMonograph.module.css` and `DrugMonographContents.jsx` define the presentation. At initial inspection the local audit-branch and production-main copies matched. APAP content remains unchanged. Concurrent production commit `73067d3bcea712ad49ceb2e87d0884c547031b31` removed the shared renderer’s default-open state; it was preserved by fast-forwarding this candidate. Original and current renderer hashes are recorded in `completion-audit.json`; this task has not edited the renderer.

Live APAP was inspected on desktop and at 390 × 844. The desktop composition is a dark page, warm off-white heading/body hierarchy, muted captions and restrained gold accents. This website blueprint is separate from the white NaS research-paper style; no paper was produced. Do not independently recolor the approved drug page.

Hero: library back link, Drug reference eyebrow, name, synonym, introductory scope paragraph, exactly three fact columns, source-check date and references link. Essential safety callout follows. The article occupies the left column; the contents rail sits right and sticks at 112px. At widths at or below 800px, the rail moves above the article and becomes a collapsible menu; mobile content margins are 20px.

Exact article order (numbered 01–09):

1. **Indications** (`indications`): oral indications; additional route/formulation where relevant.
2. **Dosage and administration** (`dosage`): named-product dose table; additional formulation; pediatric product/population; renal and hepatic considerations. APAP's doses are not a reusable clinical template.
3. **Safety** (`safety`): warnings/precautions; contraindications; boxed-warning status distinguished by product/route; adverse reactions. The warning card uses a restrained gold rule. Current production starts all disclosure cards collapsed; original data open flags remain, but the shared renderer no longer applies them.
4. **Drug interactions** (`interactions`): clinically relevant interactions and actions, citing the supporting source.
5. **Use in specific populations** (`populations`): pregnancy; lactation; pediatric and geriatric considerations.
6. **Clinical pharmacology** (`pharmacology`): mechanism/pharmacodynamics; pharmacokinetics and pertinent caveats.
7. **Monitoring and counseling** (`practice`): monitoring parameters; patient counseling information.
8. **Product identification** (`product`): exact representative product; dosage forms/strengths; storage/handling. Appearance, imprint, NDC and manufacturer belong to the selected product. Link source images; do not invent pill imagery.
9. **References** (`references`): numbered originals with publisher, exact title, URL and scope/version note.

Each clinical section has its own summary and clinical-focus takeaway. Disclosure cards use native `details`/`summary`; content may use paragraphs, bullet lists, two-column facts, badge, accessible scrolling table and external source links. Every card has numbered anchor citations. The rail highlights the current section and changes its clinical-focus text. References are generated by the shared renderer; do not add a second source-panel aesthetic.

## Batch 1 scope issues identified during initial source reading

- Atorvastatin: selected Lipitor label, Viatris set ID `a60cc18b-0631-4cf0-b021-9f52224ece65`. Acute liver failure/decompensated cirrhosis contraindications, indication-specific pediatric dosing and interaction-specific limits require explicit handling. Pregnancy is not to be inserted into the current contraindications list solely from an older statin label.
- Levothyroxine: Synthroid set ID `1e11ad30-1041-4520-10b0-8f9d30d30fcc`. Microgram units, weight/age/cardiac distinctions, TSH versus free T4 in central hypothyroidism, absorption spacing and biotin assay interference require review. Current label must not be silently replaced by an older repackager label.
- Metformin: Laurus combined IR/ER label `c3dfa8a1-d10a-4a1a-8eba-5f4e2a5a2949`. IR, ER and pediatric limits differ; eGFR thresholds, contrast holds, B12 monitoring and lactic-acidosis risks need full reconciliation.
- Amlodipine: Norvasc/Viatris `7367289c-b0b0-466a-83e2-558e2985c29f`. Adult, frail/hepatic and age 6–17 dosing differ. Liquid products require their own formulation labels.
- Lisinopril: Zestril `838c2d78-d2d8-4981-9ec9-e50ef9e1a5d8`. Hypertension, HF and acute-MI regimens; renal starting-dose changes; fetal toxicity; ACE-inhibitor angioedema; 36-hour neprilysin-inhibitor separation require explicit treatment.
- Albuterol: Ventolin HFA/GSK `d92c5d6b-ff10-4087-36a2-1cfc464cb967`. This product is 90 mcg albuterol base per actuation, age ≥4; do not reuse its dose/device instructions for nebulizers or other inhalers. GINA 2026 recommends ICS-containing asthma therapy for adults, adolescents and children 6–11 rather than SABA alone. Label indications and guideline context require separate citations.
- Losartan: Cozaar/Organon `9949448f-c3b9-44ee-94ed-c1aca8c90f39`. Hypertension, LVH/stroke and proteinuric diabetic-nephropathy scopes differ. The label qualifies stroke-benefit evidence for Black patients; do not suppress that qualification. Hepatic starting dose, pediatric eligibility and fetal toxicity require review.
- Metoprolol: current Lopressor oral label `b5f4fed2-369c-4808-a682-8a5b8cfdbb4f` is MI-specific. Toprol-XL `991fe00b-498b-400e-9e5b-921cb07d9b2c` has distinct succinate ER dosing/indications. Current generic tartrate and IV labels have now been reconciled. A repackager section 16 erroneously describing metformin was excluded and a coherent manufacturer label selected instead. **Do not narrow the ingredient-level profile solely to a selected brand's label.** Abrupt-discontinuation precautions remain important whether or not a selected label formats them as a boxed warning.
- Rosuvastatin: Crestor/AstraZeneca `325a5d0e-9a72-4015-9fcd-1655fb504cee` has September 2026 SPL effective time. Check current CV indication wording, pediatric age/diagnosis distinctions, Asian initial dosing, severe-renal limits, and individual interaction caps against this version rather than older summaries.
- Omeprazole: Prilosec prescription suspension `b6761f84-53ac-4745-a8c8-1e5427d7e179` and Prilosec OTC `37291cab-e350-d8e3-e063-6294a90a9cb1` are distinct scopes. Other capsule products need their own label. H. pylori regimen recommendations need current professional guidance; do not infer contemporary preferred antibiotic treatment from an older label alone. Clopidogrel/rilpivirine interactions and duration/population limits require full review.

These initial scope issues were reconciled in the first ten authored profiles and their individual assertion audits. The independent APAP preservation review established no confirmed material factual error, but flagged missing pediatric stop-use counseling (pain beyond five days and severe/persistent sore throat) for founder review. APAP remains byte-for-byte unchanged.
